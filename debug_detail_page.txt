Job Description - Head of Procurement (261834)
 
Head of Procurement-261834
 
Primary Location Belgium-Brussels
NATO Body NATO Communications and Information Agency (NCIA)
Schedule Full-time
Application Deadline 03-Jan-2027, 10:59:00 PM
Salary (Pay Basis) : 11,150.91Euro (EUR) Monthly
Grade NATO Grade G22
 
Description: 
 

Who We Are

The NCIA exists for one reason: to ensure the Alliance can communicate securely and reliably, whether in critical political consultations or during complex military operations. We are a key enabler of NATO’s core mission to keep one billion people safe.

Our technology and cyber experts power NATO’s digital capabilities. From data-driven decision-making and secure networks to resilient operations and mission execution across land, air, sea, cyber and space domains.

Our people make this mission possible. Join a team of 3,500 civilian and military staff across 30 locations throughout Europe, North America and Asia. 

The Role Impact

As Head of Procurement, you will be one of the Agency’s senior acquisition leaders, shaping the strategic direction, governance and transformation of procurement across the NCIA.

This is more than a procurement leadership position. It is an opportunity to influence how NATO acquires the technologies and services that underpin the Alliance’s operational readiness and future capabilities. Reporting directly to the Chief of Acquisition, you will lead a geographically dispersed and multidisciplinary procurement organization responsible for delivering complex, high-value acquisition programs in support of NATO operations, missions and strategic priorities. 

You will provide strategic advice to executive leadership, strengthen acquisition governance and lead the modernization and digitalization of procurement. By building trusted relationships across NCIA, NATO, member Nations and industry, you will help create a more agile, transparent and innovative procurement function that delivers measurable value for the Alliance. 

What You’ll Do

•    Embody and role model the NCIA culture principles of Customer First, Together and Impact
•    Lead with the customer at the center of every decision, build bridges across organizational pillars and drive delivery with the ownership and pace the mission demands
•    Build and lead a cohesive, multicultural and multidisciplinary team, promoting excellence, the highest professional standards and inspiring work environments that enable managers and staff to develop their full potential
•    Set the strategic direction for procurement and provide executive oversight across the full acquisition lifecycle
•    Oversee complex, high-value acquisition programs and major source selections
•    Direct the planning, execution and continuous improvement of Agency contracting operations
•    Develop and implement procurement policies, procedures and governance frameworks
•    Lead the modernization and digitalization of procurement through automation, artificial intelligence, data analytics and emerging acquisition solutions
•    Introduce innovative acquisition strategies and commercial practices that accelerate capability delivery while maintaining transparency and sound governance
•    Establish performance objectives and use acquisition data and metrics to improve organizational performance
•    Simplify procurement processes, reduce acquisition lead times and foster a culture of continuous improvement

For a full list of duties, please review the job description on the NCIA career site.

Who You Are

•    A senior procurement or acquisition leader with a Master’s degree and at least ten years of progressively responsible professional experience, or a Bachelor’s degree and at least fifteen years of relevant experience
•    An experienced leader of large, multidisciplinary and geographically dispersed procurement organizations
•    Able to hold a nationally or internationally recognized contracting certification, or obtain one within 24 months of taking up the position
•    Proven in developing acquisition strategies, procurement policies and effective governance frameworks
•    Experienced in overseeing major acquisition programs and complex, high-value procurements
•    A successful leader of organizational transformation, enterprise-wide change and business process improvement
•    Knowledgeable about procurement legislation, contracting policy, compliance, risk management and governance
•    Experienced in advising executive leadership on strategic acquisition and commercial matters
•    Able to modernize procurement through digital technologies, automation, data analytics and innovative contracting approaches
•    Skilled at building trusted relationships and aligning priorities across senior leaders, customers, legal and finance functions, operational stakeholders and industry
•    A decisive and accountable leader who can operate effectively in a complex international environment
•    Committed to building a diverse, inclusive and high-performing culture based on collaboration, integrity and continuous improvement

If you are a strategic leader who thrives in complex environments and is passionate about transforming procurement to deliver greater agility, transparency and operational impact, this is a unique opportunity to influence how NATO acquires the capabilities needed to meet today’s challenges and prepare for the future.

What Do We Offer?

•    Genuinely meaningful work as part of the most successful alliance in history
•    An initial five-year contract with a competitive monthly tax-free salary and household and children’s allowances
•    Privileges for staff, including expatriation and home leave where applicable, as well as education allowances
•    Excellent private health insurance
•    Generous annual leave of 30 days plus official holidays
•    Retirement Pension Plan
